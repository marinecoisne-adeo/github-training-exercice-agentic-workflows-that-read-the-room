### Workflow ideas from Awesome Copilot

Source: [Awesome Copilot workflows](https://awesome-copilot.github.com/workflows/).

- `daily-issues-report`: a daily summary of repository issues.
- `ospo-stale-repos` and `ospo-org-health`: keep an eye on stale repositories and organization health.
- `ospo-release-compliance-checker`: check releases against compliance expectations.
- `weekly-comment-sync`: keep weekly comments in sync.
